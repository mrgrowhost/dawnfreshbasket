The challenge & our response

Good food shouldn’t be this hard to find.

01

The problem

Busy households and institutions across Nakuru want fresh, safe, convenient produce — but market runs eat into time nobody has. Meanwhile, local smallholder farmers face unreliable markets and low returns on what they grow.

  • Time-consuming market trips for busy families and offices
  • Inconsistent supply for institutions that need to plan ahead
  • Farmers with good produce but unstable, unfair markets
02

Our solution

Dawn Fresh Basket sources directly from local farmers, checks every batch for quality, and delivers on a schedule that fits your household or institution — ordered over WhatsApp, paid by M-Pesa, no app required.

Payments & reconciliation

Clear timing. Easy matching.

How money moves — and how we match M-Pesa to your order.

Airtime

Paid in full now

Your custom airtime amount is paid entirely upfront with M-Pesa. We top up the number only after the confirmation SMS is matched to your order reference. Never part of the 50% deposit.

Basket deposit

50% now · 50% on delivery

If you choose priority delivery, pay half of basket + delivery now. The rest is collected on delivery. Deposit never applies to airtime.

Order reference

DFB-YYMMDD-XXXX

Every WhatsApp order includes a unique reference. Quote it when you forward your M-Pesa SMS so we can reconcile in seconds.

Reconciliation

Match → release

We match amount + reference + phone. Then: mark paid, release priority dispatch, and process airtime top-up. Automated Daraja matching can be added when you connect a backend.
